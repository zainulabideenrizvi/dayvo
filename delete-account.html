Delete your Dayvo account.

You can delete your Dayvo account and all of its data at any time, from inside the app or by sending us a request if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1 · fastest

In the app

  1. Open Dayvo and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Go to Settings → Account.
  4. Tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete forever.

Your account and data are deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Option 2 · without the app

Send a request

  1. Email mzainulabideen.rizvi@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Dayvo account".
  2. Send it from the Google email address you use to sign in to Dayvo.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you, then delete the account within 7 days and email you when it's done.
Request deletion
What happens

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes everything linked to it:

Account and profile Your name, email address, profile picture link, Google account identifier, preferences and approximate region (country and city).
Everything you created Tasks, projects, calendar events, timetables and their slots, reminders, repeat rules, completion history and productivity insights.
Google Calendar connection The encrypted authorisation token, your calendar list and all imported Google events.
Notifications Your notification inbox, scheduled reminders and device push tokens. Timetable reminders scheduled on the phone are removed when you sign out or delete your account.
Sessions and activity Sign-in sessions on every device (including their IP address and device information) and your daily activity records.
Retention

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Data is removed from our live database immediately. Encrypted backups kept by our database provider may still hold copies for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten. Backups are never used to restore a deleted account.
  • Short-lived server logs (which may include an IP address and request times, never your task or event content) are deleted within 30 days.
  • A record that an administrator action or a deletion request took place may be kept where needed for security or legal reasons. It does not contain your content.
  • Deleting your Dayvo account does not delete your Google account or anything stored in Google Calendar. To remove Dayvo's access from Google as well, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deletion is permanent. A deleted account and its data cannot be recovered. If you sign in again later, a new, empty account is created.

Keep your account

Delete some data instead

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove data:

  • Tasks, projects and events: open the item and tap Delete. It is removed from our database.
  • Google Calendar data: Settings → Calendar → Disconnect removes the authorisation token and hides imported events.
  • Notifications: turn reminder types off in Settings → Notifications.
  • Anything else: email mzainulabideen.rizvi@gmail.com and tell us what you'd like removed.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
