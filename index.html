Transparency

How Dayvo uses your Google account

  • Google Sign-In is the only way to sign in. We receive your name, email address and profile picture to create your account. We never see your password.
  • Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Dayvo gets read-only access so your events appear in Dayvo's calendar views. Dayvo never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google Calendar, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models. Dayvo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
