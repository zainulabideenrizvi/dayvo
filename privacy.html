01

Who we are

Dayvo ("Dayvo", "we", "us") is a mobile planner for tasks, projects and calendars, published by an independent developer. This policy applies to the Dayvo apps for Android and iOS, the Dayvo API that powers them, and this website.

For the purposes of data protection law, the Dayvo developer is the controller of the personal information described here. You can reach us at mzainulabideen.rizvi@gmail.com.

02

Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. Most of it is information you type into Dayvo yourself.

Account information from Google Dayvo uses Google Sign-In only. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address, profile picture link and a Google account identifier. We use these to create and recognise your account. We never see or store your Google password.
Content you create Tasks (title, notes, priority, status, due date, start time, duration, reminders, repeat rules and completion history), projects (name, colour and icon) and calendar events (title, description, location, start and end time, category, attendees you add, reminders and repeat rules) and timetables (plan name, daily or weekly layout, and each time slot's title, times, days, colour, icon, note and reminder setting).
Preferences Your theme, time zone, first day of the week, default task duration and notification settings (task reminders, event reminders, overdue alerts and the daily summary time).
Google Calendar data (optional) Only if you choose to connect Google Calendar: the list of your calendars (names and identifiers), the events on the calendars you select (title, time, description, location and attendees), the connected account's email address, and an authorisation token that lets us keep the import up to date. See Google user data below.
Notifications Timetable reminders (15 minutes before and when each slot starts) are scheduled on your device by the phone's own notification system; no server sends them. If you allow notifications for other reminders, we store a push token for your device so they reach it, along with the in-app notification inbox (for example "Task due in 15 minutes") and whether each one has been read.
Device and technical information For each signed-in device we keep a session record containing the IP address, the device and app description (user agent) and when the session was created and last used. The app sends your device's time zone with each request so dates are shown correctly. We record the calendar days on which you used the app (a date only, no activity detail), and our servers keep short operational logs (request time, route, status and errors) with credentials removed.
Approximate region (country and city) To show your country's holidays, each time you open the app we work out your approximate country and city. It comes from your network (our hosting provider estimates it from your IP address), or else from your phone's time zone and language settings. We store only the country, city and when it was worked out, not the IP address used. Dayvo never asks for or uses your device location (GPS). You can choose a different country in Settings → Calendar.
Insights we compute From your tasks we compute statistics such as completion rate, streaks and daily activity totals. These are derived from your own content and shown only to you.

Stored only on your device

Your sign-in tokens are kept in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). The app also stores your theme, whether you have seen onboarding, and a cached copy of your profile, so it can open quickly and work briefly offline.

03

What we don't collect

Dayvo does not ask for, collect or access:

  • GPS or precise device location
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos, camera or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Payment or financial details
  • Health or fitness data
  • Messages or SMS

Dayvo contains no advertising and no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

04

How we use information

We use your information only to run Dayvo and keep it safe:

  • Provide the service: create your account, sign you in, and store and sync your tasks, projects and calendar across your devices.
  • Show your calendar: display imported Google Calendar events next to your own plans, in your time zone.
  • Send reminders you ask for: task and event reminders, overdue alerts and the optional daily summary, at the times you choose.
  • Show your insights: the dashboard, activity chart and analytics screen.
  • Keep Dayvo secure and reliable: detect misuse and suspicious sign-ins (for example, reuse of a revoked session), enforce rate limits, fix bugs and measure performance.
  • Understand overall usage: aggregate counts such as daily active users and total tasks created, used to plan improvements. These never include the content of your tasks or events.
  • Communicate with you: reply to support requests and tell you about important changes to the service or to this policy.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

05

Google user data

Dayvo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we access

  • Google Sign-In (openid, email, profile): your name, email address, profile picture and account identifier, used to create and sign in to your Dayvo account.
  • Google Calendar, read-only and optional: only after you tap Connect Google Calendar in Settings and approve Google's consent screen. We read your calendar list and the events on the calendars you select, so they appear in Dayvo's day, week, month and year views.

How we use and protect it

  • Google Calendar events are shown read-only. Dayvo never creates, edits or deletes anything in your Google Calendar.
  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above. It is not used for advertising, including retargeting, personalised or interest-based ads.
  • We do not sell Google user data, and we do not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide the service (our hosting providers, see section 07), to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • No person reads your Google data unless you give explicit permission for a specific item (for example, when you ask for support), it is needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, it is required by law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • The Google authorisation token is encrypted at rest (AES-GCM) and is never returned to the app or shown in the admin tools.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time in Settings → Calendar. Disconnecting deletes the stored authorisation token and hides imported events. You can also revoke Dayvo's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting your Dayvo account removes all imported Google data.

06

Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide Dayvo to you, including your account, content, sync and reminders.
  • Consent: for optional features you switch on, such as the Google Calendar connection and push notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app or in your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the service, prevent abuse, fix problems and understand aggregate usage, in ways that do not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.
07

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share it only with the service providers that run Dayvo for us, and only as much as each one needs:

MongoDB Atlas (MongoDB, Inc.) Database hosting, where your account and content are stored.
Vercel, Inc. Hosts the Dayvo API; processes requests and short-lived server logs.
GitHub, Inc. Hosts this website (GitHub Pages), which may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
Google LLC Google Sign-In and, if you connect it, Google Calendar.
Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), Apple and Google Deliver push notifications through Expo's push service, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging. They receive your device's push token and the reminder text.

We may also disclose information:

  • when required by law, regulation or a valid legal request;
  • to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or Dayvo;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply and we will notify you first; or
  • with your consent.

Access by the Dayvo team

A small number of authorised administrators can see account-level information (name, email address, profile picture, sign-up and last sign-in dates, active sessions with device and IP address, and the number of tasks, projects and events) to provide support and keep the service secure. The admin tools do not show the content of your tasks, projects or events, and every administrator action is recorded in an audit log.

08

Where data is stored and transferred

Dayvo's servers and our service providers may be located in the United States or other countries that may not have the same data protection laws as your country. When we transfer personal information internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the providers' standard contractual clauses, and we protect it as this policy describes.

09

How long we keep data

  • Account and content: kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete something in the app, it is removed from our database.
  • Sign-in sessions: expire after 30 days without use and are then deleted automatically. Signing out ends the session immediately.
  • Google Calendar authorisation: deleted when you disconnect Google Calendar or delete your account.
  • Server logs: kept for a short period, normally no longer than 30 days, unless needed to investigate a security incident.
  • After account deletion: your data is removed from our live database straight away. Encrypted backups kept by our database provider may retain copies for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten.
10

Security

We protect your information with measures that include:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for every connection between the app and our servers;
  • short-lived access tokens (15 minutes) and refresh tokens that rotate on every use, with automatic revocation if a used token is replayed;
  • storing only a hash of each refresh token, never the token itself;
  • keeping tokens on your device in the operating system's secure storage;
  • encrypting the Google Calendar authorisation token at rest;
  • isolating every user's data by account, so one account can never read another's content;
  • removing credentials from logs, rate limiting, and restricting and auditing administrator access.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

In the app

  • Edit or delete any task, project or event at any time.
  • Turn individual reminder types on or off in Settings → Notifications, or block notifications in your device settings.
  • Connect or disconnect Google Calendar, and choose which calendars to show, in Settings → Calendar.
  • Sign out, or delete your account, in Settings → Account.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy in a portable format;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • know we do not sell or share your personal information for targeted advertising;
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights; and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email mzainulabideen.rizvi@gmail.com from the address linked to your Dayvo account. We may need to confirm your identity, and we will reply within 30 days (or sooner if your local law requires it).

12

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from the app at any time: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, tasks, projects, events, completion history, insights, notifications, preferences, sign-in sessions, activity records and any Google Calendar connection and imported events. It cannot be undone.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below these ages. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
